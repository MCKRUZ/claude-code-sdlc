1. Name and pronunciation

The product is Tōgō. Say TOH-goh. The name is the Japanese word 統合, integration. It names two things together: the claude-code-sdlc plugin and its Electron desktop front end, formerly SDLC Studio.

WrittenTōgō. Capital T, lower-case ō g ō. Both macrons, always.
SpokenTOH-goh. Two long vowels, even stress.
Origin統合 (tōgō), integration. The plugin integrates specification, checking, compliance and delivery into one executable lifecycle.
Refers toThe plugin and the desktop app as one product. In code, the app's folder is still studio/ and the renderer bridge is window.studio; the screens, window title and packaged app name are Tōgō.
Slugtogo. ASCII, lower-case, for paths and file names only: docs/brand/togo/, togo.ico, togo-tile-512.png. The slug is a file name. It is not the name.

The macrons are part of the name. Never write Togo. Never write TOGO or TŌGŌ. If a system cannot render a macron, write the name in a system that can, or use the slug and say so.

The mark is the letter ō. The bar is the macron. The disc is the bowl. Two shapes carry the one letter the name repeats.

2. The mark

Mark A, the macron, is the primary mark. It is in the product: the window icon, the tile, the lockup, the favicon. Marks B and C exist as files and PNG exports. They are not used anywhere.

The solid mark is the mark. It has one sanctioned hero treatment, Depth: the same two shapes filled from a single gradient (§2 "Mark A — Depth"). Depth lives in exactly four places — the Welcome hero, the opening card, the app icon and the steering-mode title lockup — and nowhere else.

Mark A — primary

Mark A — Depth

The hero treatment. Bar and disc are filled from one linear gradient drawn in the mark's own units — from the bar's top-left corner (17, 9) to the disc's bottom-right tangent (47, 56) — so both shapes read as one object lit from one side, never as two tinted pieces. The stops are two steps of the one accent, and they follow the theme: on light grounds accent 300 → accent 900, on dark grounds #6FD1D4 → #0E7C86 — the light ramp's 300 and the shared 600, named by hex because the dark ramp runs the other way (its own 300 is a deep teal) — so the dark mark stays luminous rather than sinking into the surface. Depth carries no meaning. It is allowed at 48 px and above, in the four hero places only (§4; the steering lockup joined them with the command center): never on a control, a chip, a status, a data body or behind text — there the solid mark stays.

Alternates, not in use

The lens reads as an instrument: an observatory watching a system it does not control. The seam reads as two halves joined. Both are kept so the owner can choose. Neither appears in the product, in documents, or in the app's assets folder.

3. Construction and clear space

The mark is drawn on a 64-unit grid. Two filled shapes, no strokes, one vertical axis at x = 32.

ElementGeometry (64-unit grid)
Bar (macron)rect x=17 y=9 width=30 height=7 rx=3.5. Spans x 17–47, y 9–16.
Disc (bowl)circle cx=32 cy=38.5 r=17.5. Spans x 14.5–49.5, y 21–56.
Gap5 units between bar bottom (16) and disc top (21).
Bounding box35 wide, 47 tall: x 14.5–49.5, y 9–56. The artwork sits 9 from the top of the canvas and 8 from the bottom.
Clear space7 units (one bar height) outside the bounding box. Nothing enters it: no text, no other mark, no container edge.
ScalingScale the whole canvas. Never change the bar-to-disc ratio or the gap.

Tile and lockup

The tile is a separate drawing, not the mark scaled. A 64 square with corner radius 14 in accent 600; bar 26 × 6, corner radius 3, at (19, 13); disc radius 15 at (32, 38); both white. The inset gives the icon room at 16 px and keeps the bowl off the rounded corner.

The lockup places the mark on its own 64-unit canvas and starts the wordmark at x 80, baseline y 46, 48 units tall. The wordmark's origin sits 33 units right of the bar's edge (47); the T's own side bearing adds a little more. On a dark ground use the white mark and wordmark-white.svg; the gap does not change.

5. Colour

One accent hue, a teal–cyan. The accent carries identity and focus. Four semantic colours carry meaning and do not change between light and dark.

One accent, semantic colours mean one thing each. Green is always signed. Amber is always a warning. Red is always an error. Sky is always running. If the accent were green, a signed stage and a brand button would look the same. The accent is therefore a hue none of the four use, and it never carries status.

7. Voice

Tōgō is an instrument. It reports what it sees and says what it did not measure. The writing follows.

  • Honest. "no data" is text, never a zero. A missing value says what would produce one: "no data. Run sprint.py close on a sprint to see rework."
  • The plugin's words, not Studio's. READY and NOT READY. Kept, carried, dropped. "Signed off by Priya Nair." "Phase 0: Discovery." The desktop app shows what the plugin reports and does not rename it, round it, or draw a shape from its prose.
  • Short declarative sentences. One idea per sentence. Tables where they help.
  • Named humans. Every decision names who made it. An AI is never the actor. A name that reads as one is refused.
  • Counts, never scores. Specs slated, business days waited, who holds the next action. Never velocity, story points, PR count, lines of code, or a per-person total.
  • Calm. No exclamation marks in the product. No marketing adjectives. The setup confirmation block in the plugin ends with one "!" and that is the budget.

Signature motion

The mark moves twice in the desktop app and nowhere else: once when the app first opens (the assemble), and once on the card that covers the window while a project opens (the draw-in). The draw-in is one gesture in three beats under 0.9 s — the bar widens from its own centre, the disc's outline is traced, the disc fills — then the mark rests; the ring beside the clock, not the mark, is what keeps saying "still working". The gesture is the brand's; the shape is not a canvas for it. Another element (a rail station, a plate) may widen-trace-fill once as it arrives. Nothing may draw the macron and disc that is not the mark, and the mark never loops, pulses or spins.

8. Do and don't

Do

  • Use mark A in flat accent 600, ink 1, or white. The solid mark is the mark.
  • Keep the 64-unit proportions and the clear space.
  • Use the tile for the favicon and app icon — flat at 16 and 32 px, Depth from 64 px; the bare mark from 24 px.
  • Use Depth only at 48 px and above, only as a hero: the Welcome hero, the opening card, the app icon. Always the one construction (§2), both shapes from the one gradient.
  • Set the name with both macrons in every language and every system that can render them.
  • Set the wordmark in Inter 650 at −0.02em, or use the SVG.
  • Pair every status colour with the plugin's word for it.

Don't

  • Do not outline the mark, add a stroke, or place it inside a second shape other than the tile.
  • Do not stretch, skew, or change the bar-to-disc ratio.
  • Do not place the mark on a photograph, a gradient, or a patterned ground. (Depth puts the gradient in the mark, never under it.)
  • Do not use a second gradient mechanism — no 3D mesh, no shadow, no bevel. Depth is the one treatment and has the one construction.
  • Do not use marks B or C anywhere until the owner chooses.
  • Do not animate the mark except its two signature moments in the desktop app: the first-open assemble and the opening card's draw-in (the bar widens from its centre, the disc's outline is traced, then fills). The gesture — widen, trace, fill — may be reused by other elements; the shape may not (§7, Signature motion).
